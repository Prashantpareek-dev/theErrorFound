About us

From one desk to a team that only does Meta ads

The Error Found started at a single desk with one client's ad account and a simple habit: open Ads Manager, find what is broken, and fix it before spending another rupee.

We kept watching brands pay for impressions that never became orders. So we dropped SEO, websites and every other ad platform, and kept only Meta ads.

That desk is now a small team of specialists. The habit hasn't changed. We audit first, show you the numbers in your own account, and only scale what already makes money.
